I support calibrating MyoTurtle at the start of each session. In three tests with the same participant, we recorded peak amplitudes of 1.60, 1.24, and 1.22 V. 
That difference made me question of like a fixed voltage would give the player a consistent target. Measuring rest and contraction before each session lets us 
adjust the LED thresholds to the signal we actually record. What I doubt is that one squeeze gives us a maximum. Besomi et al. (2020) describe several ways to
choose an EMG reference. I would compare one calibration squeeze with three repeated squeezes to see which gives more consistent thresholds.
