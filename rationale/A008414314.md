##I support the choice of section 7 because, for two classes, time domain amplitude is simple and cheap enough for the ESP32. 
A paper found that time domain features slightly more accurate than frequency domain (94.8% vs 93.2%) and is much faster (26 ms vs 198 ms
per windo), which is important for real time feedback. Normalizing to each user's calibrated maximum also keeps scores comparable across sections.

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2411.15533v3#2
