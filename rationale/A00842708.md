I support using the EMG envelope as the signal for the model. I think it makes it easier to tell rest, noise and muscle activation apart, because each one shows up as a different level. I also doubt that our number of recordings is enough to train the model well. We have 10 sessions from one person, and the model could learn the habits of one electrode placement instead of the movement itself. A review of EMG onset detection methods says that detecting muscle activation is hard when the signal is weak, so more varied recordings would help. Source: Carvalho et al. Review of electromyography onset detection methods for real-time control of robotic exoskeletons. J NeuroEng Rehabil 2023;20:141.
